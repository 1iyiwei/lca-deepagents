## **Hybrid Rendering Pipelines**
Modern engines blend rasterization, ray tracing, and neural techniques dynamically to balance performance vs. quality—optimizing for different hardware tiers automatically.

## **Gaussian Splatting (as NeRF Replacement)**
Much faster training and rendering than neural radiance fields, with better real-time performance. Bridges photogrammetry with interactive rendering and is increasingly used for scene capture and asset creation.

## **Advanced Material & Lighting Systems**

## **Animation & Character Innovation**
Physics-based character animation, AI-enhanced mocap from video, and neural animation representations are making animations more natural and efficient.

## **WebGPU & Cross-Platform Tools**
WebGPU maturation enables high-performance graphics in browsers. Tools like NVIDIA RTX Remix demonstrate practical real-time path tracing for upgrading legacy content.
